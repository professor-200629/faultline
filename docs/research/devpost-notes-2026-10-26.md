# Devpost pages: reading notes (2026-10-26)

Paraphrased notes taken while reading the official pages in a browser on
2026-10-26 (IST evening). Short quotes are marked. The pages are authoritative
and may change. Re-read them before submitting.

## https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/ (main page)

- Sidebar deadline: "Oct 30, 2026 @ 10:30pm GMT+5:30"; online; public.
- Requirement: submissions must run on Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud
  and use at least one NVIDIA open source model.
- Four tracks: Coding and Agentic Engineering; Best Apps and Agents; Personal
  AI; Physical AI. Physical AI covers embodied and edge agents (robotics, IoT,
  on-device intelligence) powered by Nemotron, GR00T, Cosmos and Sonic models,
  coordinated through an agent runtime. Nebius Serverless Jobs (simulations,
  synthetic data, policy evaluation, sensor processing) and Serverless
  Endpoints are suggested. For Physical AI, the video must include at least a
  1-minute clip of the hardware operating or, with no hardware, of the key
  application modules in action.
- What to submit: a working project; a category; a description; a working demo
  URL (not required for Physical AI); a public YouTube demo video of 3 minutes
  or less showing Token Factory and Nemotron (or other NVIDIA open models) in
  use; a public repo (GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket) with an open-source license
  visible at the top of the repo page; a README with setup instructions that
  highlights the NVIDIA model use, where Token Factory accelerated the workflow,
  and other Nebius tools used; feedback on Token Factory, AI Cloud and NVIDIA
  tools; a written explanation if the project existed before the submission
  period.
- Judging criteria (stated as questions): Technological Implementation (how
  well it is built and how effectively it uses Token Factory or AI Cloud models
  and Nemotron); Design (a complete, coherent product experience, not just a
  proof of concept); Potential Impact (a credible, specific case for a real
  problem and a real audience, addressed by what is demonstrated); Quality of
  the Idea (a creative, non-obvious use, and genuine understanding of the
  problem space).
- Prizes listed: Grand $20,000; 2nd $10,000; 3rd $6,000; each track winner an
  NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano; Best Use of Tavily $3,000; City Winner Awards
  (20 x $500); Most Valuable Feedback (10 x $100).

## https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules

- Submission Period: Aug 26, 2026 9:00 am PT to Oct 30, 2026 10:00 am PT.
  Judging Period: Dec 1 to Dec 15, 2026. Winners around Jan 11, 2027.
- Sponsor: Nebius B.V. Administrator: Devpost, Inc.
- Eligibility excludes residents of Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran,
  North Korea and other comprehensively sanctioned regions.
- "Runs on Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud" means a runtime call to the
  Token Factory inference API, or deployment on Nebius AI Cloud compute
  (Serverless Jobs, Serverless Endpoints or DevPods).
- New and existing projects: a pre-existing project must have been
  significantly updated during the period, with an explanation.
- Submission requirements: a demo URL (not required for Physical AI, which is
  evaluated under the hardware-access provision); a text description; a public
  repository with all source, assets and instructions, and an open-source
  license file detectable and visible at the top of the repository page (in
  the About section); a README with setup instructions and the NVIDIA, Token
  Factory and Nebius highlights. The video should be under three minutes
  (judges are not required to watch beyond three minutes), should show the
  project functioning, must be public on YouTube, and must not use third-party
  trademarks or copyrighted music without permission. Physical AI videos need
  at least one minute of hardware operating or of the key modules in action.
  Also required: the track, platform feedback, the pre-existing-work
  explanation if any, and English materials (or translations).
- Testing: the entrant must give judges access to the working project
  (website, functioning demo or test build), free of charge and without
  restriction, until the Judging Period ends. Judges may judge on the text
  description, images and video alone.
- Judging: Stage One is pass/fail on theme fit and on reasonably applying the
  required APIs/SDKs ("a genuine attempt at the track's stated goal, not a
  superficial rebrand of an unrelated idea"). Stage Two uses four equally
  weighted criteria (as on the main page). Ties are broken by criterion order.
- IP: the submission must be the entrant's original work; open source may be
  used if the submission builds upon it and its licenses are respected.

## https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/faqs

- Some countries are not yet supported for Token Factory billing onboarding.
  This is an account-provisioning gap, not an eligibility rule. Contact the
  Nebius team on Discord.
- Calling an NVIDIA model through Token Factory satisfies the platform
  requirement. The rest of the app does not have to be hosted on Nebius AI
  Cloud. The official promotional credits (direct-credits form code
  NEBIUS-DEVPOST-GLOBAL26, and the Nebius Builders Program) are Token Factory
  credits; there is no separate AI Cloud grant.
- Physical AI may use simulation instead of a physical robot. There is no
  required hardware list, as long as the project runs on Token Factory or AI
  Cloud with at least one NVIDIA open model.
- NVIDIA open models beyond Nemotron, GR00T, Cosmos and Sonic (for example
  Alpamayo) are allowed for Physical AI.
- No maximum team size.
- Missing promo credits are handled case by case with Nebius on Discord.
- Calling an NVIDIA model through another provider (HuggingFace, OpenRouter,
  ...) does not satisfy the requirement.
